PROVENANCE — every image and film in this concept
==================================================

Rule for this build, and it has no exceptions:

  NOTHING SYNTHETIC, AND NOTHING WHOSE ORIGIN IS UNCONFIRMED.

That is why there is no `gen-` prefix anywhere in this folder: not one file
here was generated, upscaled, extended or generatively filled, so
`generated-imagery` does not bind this build at all. It is also why several
strong-looking frames in the working project were left behind — see the
bottom of this file.

Every file was COPIED, unmodified, out of the read-only working project at
/Users/alex/Desktop/WORK/Chicago Motor Cars. Nothing there was edited, moved
or renamed.
