# Dialect — expressive-poster

> **Status: library.** An art direction available as an input to a Design Read —
> not a claim about Alex's own taste, and not a house dialect. The only route to
> `confirmed` is the evidence rule in
> [vault/README.md](../vault/README.md#creating-a-new-dialect).

**Core thesis.** Type is the image: build the composition out of scale collisions,
compression and release, so that one message is unforgettable at a glance and the
page confronts rather than presents.

**Emotional character.** Immediate, physical, a little aggressive. A visitor feels
addressed at volume — and, if it is working, feels the volume was earned by having
something to say.

---

## DECISION LOGIC

**One message carries the page.** The dialect is built for a single statement. Two
statements at poster scale produce two posters and no page.

**Scale is the instrument, and it collides.** Ranks are separated by multiples, not
steps: 8× between the statement and its support is normal. The collision *is* the
composition.

**Compression earns release.** Density is deliberate and local: a compressed,
crowded passage exists so the open field after it lands. Uniform loudness is not
this dialect — it is noise.

**Confrontation is aimed, not sprayed.** Something is being confronted — a
convention, an expectation, a competitor's politeness. If nothing is, the aggression
is decoration.

**When two goods conflict, memorability wins over comfort — and never over
comprehension.** A poster that cannot be read has failed at its own job.

## SYSTEMS — what this dialect does to each domain

| Domain | This dialect's behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Composition / grid** | Poster logic: one dominant typographic mass, framed edge-to-edge. Grid exists to be violated at one declared point; crops are hard and committed |
| **Hierarchy / density** | Violent steps — the statement, then everything else. Alternating compression and open field rather than even density |
| **Typography behaviour** | **The centre of gravity.** One display voice with strong character, used enormously; a quiet functional voice for everything a visitor must actually operate. Letterforms may be cropped, overlapped, stacked, or set to the frame |
| **Spacing / rhythm** | Extreme contrast between intervals. Tight inside the statement, wide around it. Rhythm from that alternation, not from a regular step |
| **Colour / contrast logic** | High contrast, few colours, saturated where it matters. Colour is structural — figure against ground — never atmospheric |
| **Image behaviour** | Photography is subordinate to type or fused with it: cropped, masked, overlapped, treated. One image, used hard, beats four used politely |
| **Containers / borders / geometry** | Almost none. The frame and the type's own edges do the containing. Where a shape exists it is a flat field, not a card |
| **Depth / materiality** | Flat and graphic — print, not screen. Overlap gives layer order; no elevation, no ambient shadow |
| **Motion / interaction** | Sharp and few. A cut or a hard slide over an ease; motion may reveal the collision but never animates continuously |
| **Information presentation** | Deliberately plain, in the quiet voice: lists, short blocks, real controls at ordinary scale. The dialect shouts once and then speaks normally |

## SIGNATURE DEVICES

- **The scale collision** — the statement at a size that forces every other element
  to be measured against it. Its job is to make one message unforgettable.
- **The cropped letterform** — type running off the frame or cut by an image edge. Its
  job is to assert that the page continues beyond the viewport.
- **The compression block** — one tight, dense passage against an open field. Its job
  is to control pace.

As a SIGNATURE influence in another dialect's hybrid, borrow exactly one — almost
always the scale collision, **once**.

## WHAT IT AVOIDS

Everything loud at the same time; two display voices; decorative outlines and strokes
on type; drop shadows; ambient loops; navigation sacrificed to composition; and
aggression with no target.

## FALSE VERSIONS — the clichés that wear its clothes

**Loud everywhere** is the failure: every section with its own oversized headline,
three display faces, marquee bands stacked, uppercase at every rank, saturated colour
on saturated colour, and a navigation nobody can find. It has the dialect's volume
and none of its structure — because the structure *is* the contrast between loud and
quiet, and a page with no quiet has deleted it.

Tells: no quiet functional voice anywhere; more than one statement at poster scale;
the marquee used more than once; and body copy set in the display face.

Second false version: **poster as a substitute for content** — an enormous phrase
that says nothing specific, sitting above a page with no evidence, no task and no
answer to [C18](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant).

## FIT

**Good fit:** campaigns, launches, events, cultural programmes, manifestos, single-
message pages; music, fashion, sport, youth culture; audiences who scroll fast and
remember shapes.

**Poor fit:** dense functional tools; documentation; multi-message service pages;
audiences arriving with a task in a hurry; briefs whose credibility depends on
appearing measured — a bank, a clinic, an engineering supplier. Prefer
`swiss-editorial`, `technical-luxury` or `brutalist-utility`.

## PURE MODE

The whole art direction: one typographic dominant, violent steps, compression and
release, flat graphic depth, sharp motion, plain functional layer.

**It does not license:** display type carrying body-length reading
([typography I2](../skills/typography-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), navigation hidden
for composition ([C14](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant)),
untracked uppercase at length
([I4](../skills/typography-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), or contrast used as an excuse
to skip measurement. Loud and legible is the whole trick.

## HYBRID RELATIONSHIPS

**Useful**
- **+ `swiss-editorial`** — *one planned rupture inside a rigorous system.* Swiss
  anchors grid, hierarchy and density; the poster owns exactly one collision and the
  display voice. The rupture reads *because* the system is real.
- **+ `technical-luxury`** — the collision where the product meets its own name,
  against precise evidence. Keeps precision from reading as timidity.
- **+ `refined-elegance`** (as signature into refined) — one rupture in a measured
  page. Very effective, and very easy to overspend.

**Dangerous**
- **+ `brutalist-utility`** — both own scale and both want the loudest voice; one
  becomes decoration behind the other.
- **+ `cinematic-industrial`** — type-as-image against image-as-subject: two
  candidates for the dominant, and [C13](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant)
  breaks whichever loses.

## ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY

The risks are specific: enormous type reflowing badly at 320px; the quiet functional
layer shrinking below the floor because the statement took the budget
([I7](../skills/typography-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)); saturated pairs failing AA;
cropped letterforms breaking screen-reader text unless the accessible name is
intact; and reading order diverging from visual order in an overlapped composition.
Reduced motion must have an authored still, not a frozen mid-transition.

## DIAGNOSTICS

1. Is there exactly one statement at poster scale?
2. Is there a genuinely quiet functional voice?
3. What is being confronted? Name it.
4. Where is the release after the compression?
5. At 320px, does the statement still land — or does it become six lines of noise?
6. Can a visitor find and operate the primary control without hunting?

## THE LOGIC, COMPACTLY

*Brief: a two-night electronic music festival, one visual asset, ticket sales are the
task, audience is 18–30 on phones.*

One asset and one message, which is exactly this dialect's shape: the festival name
at a scale that forces the phone's frame to crop it, so the page reads as a fragment
of something bigger than the screen — that crop is the confrontation, aimed at the
politeness of every other event page. Underneath, a compression block: the full
line-up in tight, dense type at ordinary size, which lands because the field above it
is empty. Two colours, structural, high contrast. The ticket control is in the quiet
voice at a completely conventional scale, because the dialect shouts once and then
speaks normally — and the shout is worthless if the sale fails. One hard cut on the
day-switch, nothing continuous. What the dialect decided was not "make it loud" — it
was that *the name has to survive being seen for half a second in a feed*, and
everything else exists to make that possible.
