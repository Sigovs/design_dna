# auction-editorial — compositional preferences

*Read only when the Design Read has selected this dialect.*

> **This file is not the dialect.** The dialect itself — its principles, its
> optional expressions and its status — lives in
> [dialects/auction-editorial.md](../../../../dialects/auction-editorial.md),
> which is the single source of truth. This file collects only what the dialect
> tends to conclude **about composition**, so a composition task does not have to
> read the whole dialect, and it never restates a principle that file owns.

## Intended context

Auction, collector, automotive, gallery, and image-led editorial work: a small
number of high-value objects, photographed deliberately, presented to an audience
that has come to evaluate rather than to browse.

It is a **position**, not a law. Everything below yields under the conditions
listed in [SKILL.md's DIALECT section](../../SKILL.md#dialect), and the invariants
C1–C18 bind regardless.

## Risks — stated, because a dialect that hides its risks is a house style

- **Forced asymmetry.** Asymmetry applied because the dialect prefers it, with no
  counterweight. Drift wearing an editorial coat.
- **Repeated art-direction mannerisms.** The same crop, the same offset, the same
  disruption on every project. This is what
  [projects/](../../../../projects/README.md) exists to detect — the vault cannot
  see self-repetition.
- **Weak usability in dense functional work.** Air and delay are wrong for
  scanning, comparison and repeated operator tasks. In those briefs the dialect
  should not have been selected.
- **Dependency on exceptional photography.** Most of these mechanisms assume
  deliberate, consistent, high-tolerance images. With a mixed or weak library the
  composition has no dominant to build on, and the correct response is a different
  compositional system, not a better crop.
- **Excessive empty space.** Space without shape, defended as luxury. See
  [negative-space-and-tension](../negative-space-and-tension.md).
- **Every project acquiring the same voice.** The failure the two-tier model
  exists to prevent: the dialect executing an identity instead of serving one.

## When it yields

The full list is in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md#dialect). The short form: usability
needing directness, density needing compact scanning, audience expectations
favouring speed or familiarity, the brand requiring another spatial character,
cultural context, an expressive or youth-oriented register, or a brief that calls
for symmetry, modularity, playfulness or utility.

**Yielding is correct practice. Yielding silently is the failure**
([TASTE.md §1](../../../../TASTE.md#1-the-two-tiers)) — name the yield and its
condition in the report.

## What still binds when it is active

All of C1–C18. Specifically, the five this dialect most often strains:

- **[C18](../../SKILL.md#invariant)** — atmosphere arrives before identification in
  this register, and the vault records that as a weakness of its *best* entries.
  Either the first screen says what the thing is, or the Read declares that the
  withholding is the subject.

- **[C17](../../SKILL.md#invariant)** — this dialect's mechanisms assume exceptional
  photography, which is exactly the dependency C17 requires you to declare. With a
  mixed library the register has nothing to stand on.

- **[C13](../../SKILL.md#invariant)** — the object is the dominant. A committed
  gesture that outranks the car, the lot or the garment has eaten the work.
- **[C14](../../SKILL.md#invariant)** — restraint never deletes the means of the
  visitor's task. An auction page without a way to bid, or an inventory page
  without a way to search, is not quiet; it is broken.
- **[C12](../../SKILL.md#invariant)** — the counterweight holding the asymmetry has
  to exist on mobile too, or the asymmetry goes.
