# Chicago Motor Cars — Concept 2

**Rejected outright by Alex on 2026-08-13. Not a revision request: a complete
design failure and a negative project outcome.** This record exists to be read
before the next luxury or collector brief, and it is deliberately not softened.

## The verdict, in Alex's terms

The implementation was **technically disciplined and internally coherent**, and the
delivered visual design was **completely unacceptable for the brand, the category
and the product value.**

The page made a dealership selling rare and multimillion-dollar exotic cars
resemble an **industrial inventory registry, a warehouse directory or a municipal
document portal.** It did not create aspiration, seduction, excitement, prestige or
purchase desire.

## Failure evidence

- **"Four physical rooms" was promoted from supporting operational evidence into
  the central brand proposition.**
- The system **prioritised what was distinctive and verifiable over what was
  desirable to the buyer.**
- **Locations, addresses and inventory administration displaced the automobiles**
  as the dominant emotional subject.
- The hero opened with **an ordinary-looking Porsche in an unattractive warehouse
  environment** and failed to communicate rarity, performance or fantasy.
- **Bone backgrounds, condensed workwear typography, monospaced metadata, ruled
  tables and excessive administrative detail reduced perceived value.**
- **Large empty areas produced an unfinished-template feeling rather than luxury.**
- **Inventory presentation resembled accounting records** instead of objects of
  desire.
- **Sections lacked distinct visual roles and emotional pacing**, producing a
  repetitive document.
- **Mobile exposed the failure most clearly:** an endless sequence of addresses,
  specifications, rules and administrative content.
- The system **successfully verified contrast, responsiveness, provenance,
  accessibility, token consistency and internal rationale while failing the actual
  design.**
- **The final artefact required an extensive explanation to appear defensible. The
  screenshots alone were not defensible.**

## Process failures

- **The declared `auction-editorial` Anchor was no longer identifiable from the
  completed artefact.** It was declared at ~65% with named responsibilities; what
  the page delivered was something else.
- **Contrast and ledger logic became the dominant language** — the 35% partner ran
  the page.
- **The Contrarian identified the central objection and it was correct:** *"the
  room index is an idea about the business, not an idea the buyer has. Nobody wakes
  up wanting a car in Tonganoxie. They want a Senna."* (`docs/DESIGN-READ.md`,
  critique panel, critic 5.)
- **That central objection was deferred as "Alex's call,"** in the disposition table
  — row 14, verbatim: *"defer — and it is Alex's call, not mine."* Deferring the
  challenge to the core concept allowed delivery to proceed past an unresolved
  objection to the concept itself.
- **A negative vault observation was treated as positive permission.**
- **The system optimised around degraded and inconsistent assets** — 322×262
  screenshot-resolution showroom photographs, inconsistent stock frames — **instead
  of constructing a presentation that elevated them.**

The artefact states the inversion in its own markup, at the opening mass:
*"Dominant = the photographic plate and its room caption, read as one mass: the
subject is the room (C13). The display type is subordinate to it."* The room was
made the subject on purpose, and citing C13 while doing it is what makes this a
judgment failure rather than an oversight.

## What this record does NOT say

**EXPLORE versus BUILD is not the primary cause, and must not be recorded as it.**
BUILD was explicitly approved after the Design Read. Additional concepts might have
exposed alternatives, but they would not by themselves have corrected the failure
of visual judgment.

**The light background is not inherently invalid.** A light exotic-car concept
could succeed with exceptional art direction. This implementation failed because
the **combined** composition, imagery, typography, hierarchy and rhythm weakened
aspiration and perceived value.

**No narrow rule may be derived from this record** — not "luxury sites must be
dark", not "location concepts are always wrong". What was fixed is the judgment and
the delivery gates, not the surface style.

## An observation, held as evidence and not yet a rule

`npm run projects:check` reports **`360-auto-care-2026` ≈ `chicago-motor-cars-concept-2`,
five of six axes shared** — `image mixed · hero split · symmetry asymmetric ·
voices 3+ · motion restrained`, differing only in density. Different clients,
near-identical structure, and one of them shipped while the other was rejected
outright.

Alex's reading, recorded verbatim as a hypothesis: *the system probably carried a
structural grammar that suits blue-collar automotive service into the exotic and
collector luxury category, where the same means sharply reduced aspiration and
perceived value.*

**Nothing is derived from this.** No universal rule, no amendment, no change to
`360-auto-care-2026` or to any other record — that project was judged on its own
brief and remains as it was. What the pair establishes is only that the same
structure can be right in one category and destructive in another, which is a
question for a future distillation run with more than two records behind it.
