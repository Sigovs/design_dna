# explore/

Exploration artifacts from [EXPLORE](../TASTE.md#2d-explore--three-directions-before-one-is-chosen)
rounds: three structurally different directions for one brief, each proved by the
deciding screen rather than by a finished site.

```
explore/<project>-<date>/
  a.html          evidence-led
  b.html          alternative
  c.html          wildcard
  screenshots/    one per direction, desktop and mobile where it matters
```

**These are project exploration, not vault evidence.** Nothing here is a reference,
a rating, or a taste judgement. A direction Alex rejected lost a comparison on one
brief — it is not negative evidence about anything, and it does not go near
`vault/`. The outcome is recorded in the project record's
[exploration section](../projects/_TEMPLATE.md), which stays project-local unless
Alex says a judgement travels further.

**Keep them.** A rejected direction is the cheapest record of what was considered
and why it lost, and the next round starts from it rather than from nothing.

Three practical rules:

- **One key screen per direction.** Three complete websites is not exploration, it
  is three builds nobody asked for.
- **Structural difference or it does not count** — composition, hierarchy, rhythm,
  image behaviour, typography strategy, interaction model, density, symmetry, hero
  treatment, motion. Three palettes on one layout is one direction.
- **Invariants bind in all three.** Exploration is open on dialect and mood, never
  on hierarchy, contrast, legibility, or the means of the visitor's task.
