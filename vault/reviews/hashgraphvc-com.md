### The transferable lesson

**Let the object earn the sentence.** The headline does not introduce the scene; it arrives
after the scene has already made the point, and it says the same thing in words the visitor
has just watched happen. The cost is stated plainly in `weaknesses` — full scroll-jacking,
a gate, a long preloader — and that cost is only worth paying when there is one event worth
staging. Nothing here transfers to a page whose job is to be found, linked into and read.

### For the underwater scene Alex is working on

Three things this page does that are worth stealing for that brief, recorded here because
they are mechanisms rather than looks: **light is the only hierarchy** — the frame is almost
black and the eye goes where the glow is; **the object is small against the volume** and only
becomes readable as it approaches, which is what gives the water its depth; and **motion
stops**. The scene holds still between events, and the stillness is what makes the next
movement read.
