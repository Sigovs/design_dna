# Dialect — cinematic-industrial

> **Status: library.** An art direction available as an input to a Design Read —
> not a claim about Alex's own taste, and not a house dialect. The only route to
> `confirmed` is the evidence rule in
> [vault/README.md](../vault/README.md#creating-a-new-dialect).

**Core thesis.** Light, scale and material consequence do the persuading: stage the
subject as a physical object in a real space, and let the way it is lit carry the
argument that prose would otherwise have to make.

**Emotional character.** Weight, tension, engineered seriousness. A visitor feels
the thing exists, is heavy, and was made — not rendered.

---

## DECISION LOGIC

**Light is the primary instrument.** Where another dialect adds contrast or scale,
this one changes the lighting: direction, hardness, fall-off, what stays in shadow.
A decision that could be solved by lighting is solved there first.

**Darkness is controlled, not default.** The dark ground exists so that light means
something. An evenly dark page has spent its whole instrument on atmosphere and has
nothing left for emphasis — the fall-off is the design.

**Scale is claimed once.** One mass is monumental; everything else is measured
against it. Two monuments cancel.

**Materiality over effect.** Surfaces read as metal, glass, concrete, rubber, paint —
things with weight and fabrication. This is a photographic and tonal claim, not a
texture overlay.

**Mechanical rhythm.** Repetition is regular and slightly relentless, like plant
machinery: even intervals, consistent crops, a beat the reader can feel. Departures
are rare and structural.

**When two goods conflict, physical credibility wins over drama.** A dramatic frame
that misrepresents the object costs more than it buys.

## SYSTEMS — what this dialect does to each domain

| Domain | This dialect's behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Composition / grid** | Full-bleed masses, one dominant object, generous headroom above the subject. The grid serves the image rather than the reverse |
| **Hierarchy / density** | Rank by luminance and scale before size: the lit thing is first. Low-to-moderate density; information collects in compact groups against open field |
| **Typography behaviour** | Subordinate to image by design. One or two voices, tight and mechanical; uppercase used for technical registers, tracked; type placed in the image's quiet regions, not over its subject |
| **Spacing / rhythm** | Even, machine-like intervals. Space is atmospheric — it reads as air in a room, not as margin on a page |
| **Colour / contrast logic** | Narrow, low-chroma palette on a dark ground; one accent standing for energy, heat or state. Contrast spent on luminance, per [C3](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant) |
| **Image behaviour** | **The dialect's centre of gravity.** Directional hard light, deep shadow with detail, consistent grade across the library, crops that commit. Motion blur and dust are physical facts, not filters |
| **Containers / borders / geometry** | Minimal and industrial: hairlines, machined corners, no soft radii. Containers are rare — the field does the containing |
| **Depth / materiality** | Real spatial depth from light and occlusion, not from elevation shadows. Foreground, subject, and receding field are distinguishable |
| **Motion / interaction** | Heavy and eased — mass that takes time to start and stop. Camera-like movement over element animation. Slow, few, deliberate |
| **Information presentation** | Technical registers: measurements, codes, states, set compactly in mono as evidence of engineering |

## SIGNATURE DEVICES

- **The single lit object** — one subject, hard-lit against a dark field, holding the
  frame. Its job is to make the product the dominant, per
  [C13](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant).
- **The technical register** — a compact mono block of real measurements. Its job is
  to convert atmosphere into credibility.
- **Weighted motion** — one transition with visible inertia. Its job is to assert
  mass.

As a SIGNATURE influence in another dialect's hybrid, **the borrowed job is almost
always image lighting** — it touches photography and nothing else.

## WHAT IT AVOIDS

Even darkness with no fall-off; neon as a personality; smoke, sparks, lens flares
and speed lines; ambient loops behind text; type over the subject's face or body;
elevation shadows on a page already using real light; and rendering used where a
photograph is available.

## FALSE VERSIONS — the clichés that wear its clothes

**The cinematic effect stack** is the failure and it is instantly recognisable: a
black background, blue-cyan neon rim light, volumetric smoke, sparks, a slow parallax
of a rendered car, glitch text, and a headline in a wide tracked uppercase grotesque.
It is a video-game menu, and it says nothing about the object.

Tells: the light comes from nowhere identifiable; every surface is wet; the accent
is cyan or orange because those are the defaults; the darkness has no gradient of
information in it; and removing the effects leaves no composition behind.

Second false version: **atmosphere instead of evidence.** A page that feels serious
and contains no facts — no specification, no measurement, no provenance. This dialect
earns its drama with evidence; without it, the drama is a mood board and
[C17](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant) is already failing, because
the identity rests entirely on photography nobody has committed to reshooting.

## FIT

**Good fit:** physical products with real fabrication — vehicles, machinery,
instruments, equipment, architecture; performance and engineering claims; audiences
who respond to craft evidence; briefs with a genuinely good, consistent image
library.

**Poor fit:** service businesses with no object to photograph; dense functional
tools; any brief where the imagery is stock or inconsistent — the dialect has nothing
to light. Prefer `brutalist-utility` or `swiss-editorial` there, and say why.

## PURE MODE

The whole art direction: dark controlled ground, one lit dominant, subordinate
mechanical type, technical registers, weighted motion, real spatial depth.

**It does not license:** text below AA on a dark field
([color I1](../skills/color-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), legibility fixed by
guessing rather than measuring at the composited render
([color I4](../skills/color-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), ambient motion behind body
copy ([dimensionality DM9](../skills/dimensionality/SKILL.md#invariant)), or a hero
whose subject nobody can identify
([C18](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant)).

## HYBRID RELATIONSHIPS

**Useful**
- **+ `technical-luxury`** — *the object earns the drama it is given.* Cinematic owns
  light, depth and image; technical owns specification, detailing and information.
  The strongest pairing in this library for products.
- **+ `brutalist-utility`** (as signature into brutalist) — a flat, hard frame with
  photography that has real light. Keeps the structure honest and the object
  desirable.
- **+ `auction-editorial`** — *the record, lit.* Auction owns metadata, hierarchy and
  restraint; cinematic owns image light. Risk: both defer, and the page becomes
  atmosphere with a caption.

**Dangerous**
- **+ `organic-tactile`** — hard directional light against soft natural variation:
  the two treatments of material read as two photographers.
- **+ `retro-futurist`** — both reach for spectacle and period reference; together
  they produce pastiche fast.

## ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY

The characteristic risks are all on the dark ground: text over photography measured
on the source image rather than the composited render; secondary greys failing AA;
focus rings invisible against near-black; and motion with inertia becoming
uncomfortable without a reduced-motion path
([motion](../skills/motion-taste/SKILL.md#invariant) binds). Dark fields also make
the 14px functional floor harder — thin light type on black reads smaller than it
measures.

## DIAGNOSTICS

1. Can you say where the light comes from, in one clause?
2. Is there a gradient of information inside the darkness, or is it flat black?
3. Is exactly one mass monumental?
4. Remove every effect. Is a composition still there?
5. Is there any real evidence on the page — a measurement, a spec, a provenance?
6. Would the object's owner recognise the object as theirs, correctly represented?

## THE LOGIC, COMPACTLY

*Brief: a company that rebuilds vintage motorcycle engines, 20 rebuilds a year,
excellent workshop photography, buyers who know the machines.*

The buyer's question is *was this built properly*, so the light answers it: hard
directional light across machined surfaces, so tolerances and finish are visible —
the photograph is inspection, not glamour. One engine is monumental in the first
screen with headroom above it; nothing else on the page competes at that scale.
Darkness carries a fall-off from the lit case down into the shadowed bench, which is
where the technical register sits — bore, stroke, hours, serial, in mono, compact,
as evidence rather than decoration. Type stays subordinate and mechanical, placed in
the field, never over the engine. One motion with inertia when a rebuild is opened;
nothing else moves. What the dialect decided was not "make it dark and dramatic" — it
was that *the fabrication is the argument*, and lighting is the cheapest way to show
fabrication.
