# Dialect — refined-elegance

> **Status: library.** An art direction available as an input to a Design Read —
> not a claim about Alex's own taste, and not a house dialect. The only route to
> `confirmed` is the evidence rule in
> [vault/README.md](../vault/README.md#creating-a-new-dialect).

**Core thesis.** Quality is legible in proportion and interval before it is legible
in anything else: decide the relationships between masses to a fine tolerance, then
let almost nothing else happen.

**Emotional character.** Composed, unhurried, attentive. A visitor feels the work
was measured rather than assembled — and, importantly, feels *addressed* rather than
impressed. Presence without volume.

---

## DECISION LOGIC

**Proportion is the primary instrument.** When something is not working, the first
move is to change a ratio — mass to mass, text to margin, interval to interval — not
to add contrast, colour or a device.

**Restraint with presence, never restraint as absence.** The distinction that
separates this dialect from its cliché: a quiet page still has a dominant, a
directed eye path, and one thing that is unmistakably first. Emptiness without
hierarchy is not elegance; it is an unfinished page with wide margins.

**Fewer elements, each more considered.** The budget is small and every item in it
is optically corrected: tracking at size, leading per role, alignment to the same
axes, hairlines rather than boxes.

**Detail rewards attention but never demands it.** Refinement lives at the second
read: the tabular figures, the true quote marks, the hanging punctuation, the
consistent optical margin. If a visitor must notice a detail for the page to work,
it is not a detail — it is hierarchy, and it belongs at the first read.

**When two goods conflict, coherence wins over emphasis.** This dialect would
rather under-state a good thing than break the system to promote it.

---

## SYSTEMS — what this dialect does to each domain

| Domain | This dialect's behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Composition / grid** | Asymmetric but stable, held by a counterweight rather than by centring. Generous but *shaped* margins — the space has proportion and direction, per [C4](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant) |
| **Hierarchy / density** | Moderate steps, unmistakably ordered. Low density, achieved by removing content rather than by shrinking it |
| **Typography behaviour** | One display voice with real character, one quiet text voice. Optical correction at every size; measure held to a comfortable line; line breaks authored on anything display-scale |
| **Spacing / rhythm** | A fine-grained scale used precisely. Intervals between passages differ audibly from intervals inside them; rhythm develops slowly and resolves |
| **Colour / contrast logic** | Narrow palette, low chroma, tonal rather than chromatic hierarchy. Contrast spent on tone and interval; accent rare and small, and derived per [color I5](../skills/color-taste/SKILL.md#invariant) |
| **Image behaviour** | Few images, well cropped, quiet in tone. Consistent treatment throughout. Images sit in the composition rather than fill it, and they hold their own air |
| **Containers / borders / geometry** | Hairlines and alignment instead of boxes. If a radius exists it is small, single-valued and consistent. Nothing is outlined that space could group |
| **Depth / materiality** | Shallow and honest. Paper and print rather than screen elevation; at most one plane of separation |
| **Motion / interaction** | Short, eased, few. Crossfade over travel; hover shifts a state rather than performing. Focus is visible and quiet |
| **Information presentation** | Composed as a record — aligned columns, tabular figures, labels in a consistent register. Specification set with the same care as prose |

## SIGNATURE DEVICES

- **The shaped margin** — an asymmetric field of space, held at a stated ratio, that
  does the work a border would do. Its job is grouping without enclosure.
- **The hairline register** — a 1px rule carrying a small label, used to mark
  structure. Its job is to give metadata a place without a box.
- **The single optical correction that shows** — a hung quote, an aligned figure
  column, a headline whose breaks are authored. Its job is to signal that the page
  was measured.

As a SIGNATURE influence in another dialect's hybrid, borrow exactly one — most
often the hairline register.

## WHAT IT AVOIDS

Ornament; symmetry chosen to avoid deciding; more than one expressive voice; large
areas of empty space with no shape; drop shadows and elevation; radii applied for
friendliness; gradients; motion that performs; and the word "luxury" doing the work
that a proportion should do.

## FALSE VERSIONS — the clichés that wear its clothes

**Generic elegance** is the failure, and it is nearly a template: acres of
whitespace with no hierarchy, a thin light serif at 200 weight, beige on beige,
a centred everything, wide-tracked uppercase labels, a full-bleed lifestyle
photograph with a two-word caption, and nothing anywhere that ranks first.

Three tells:

1. **Nothing is first.** Squint: if no mass resolves, the "elegance" is emptiness.
2. **The palette is one value.** Beige on beige, grey on grey — no tonal structure,
   so [C3](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant) fails and the page
   dies in grayscale, which is where it already was.
3. **The type is thin instead of considered.** Weight 200 at 14px reads as fragile,
   not refined, and usually breaks
   [color I1](../skills/color-taste/SKILL.md#invariant) as well.

Second false version: **elegance as avoidance** — a page that says nothing
specific, shows no evidence and takes no position, defended as restraint. Restraint
is what you do *with* a claim, not a substitute for having one.

## FIT

**Good fit:** work sold on judgement and craft — studios, ateliers, private
practices, cultural institutions, considered products; audiences who read slowly
and are put off by being shouted at; briefs with a small quantity of very good
content.

**Poor fit:** dense functional tools; high-urgency tasks; audiences scanning on
mobile in a hurry; briefs with a large quantity of mediocre content, where the
dialect's low density will either hide it or stretch the page to nothing. Prefer
`swiss-editorial` for volume with order, `brutalist-utility` for tasks.

## PURE MODE

The whole art direction: proportion-led composition, one display voice, tonal
palette, hairlines, shallow depth, few and short motions, information composed as a
record.

**It does not license:** low contrast, thin type at small sizes, functional text
under 14px ([I7](../skills/typography-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), removing content
to keep a page quiet ([U7](../skills/anti-patterns/SKILL.md#invariant)), or a first
screen that does not say what the thing is
([C18](../skills/academic-composition/SKILL.md#invariant)). Quiet is not a reason
for any of those, and this dialect fails more often by omission than by excess.

## HYBRID RELATIONSHIPS

**Useful**
- **+ `brutalist-utility`** — the pairing this dialect is best in. Brutalist owns
  composition, geometry and information display; refined owns spacing and
  typographic detail throughout. Tension: exposure against consideration. Principle:
  *honest structure, precisely made.*
- **+ `technical-luxury`** — closely related; only pair when one clearly anchors.
  Refined owns rhythm and restraint; technical owns specification and detailing.
  Risk: they merge into one register and the contrast disappears.
- **+ `expressive-poster`** (signature) — one scale collision, once, inside an
  otherwise measured system. The rupture reads because everything else is quiet.

**Dangerous**
- **+ `organic-tactile`** — both are quiet, both own texture and rhythm; the result
  is a soft page with no tension and no anchor.
- **+ `retro-futurist`** — period mannerism inside a measured system usually reads
  as decoration, because this dialect has no appetite for the costume the pairing
  needs.

## ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY

The characteristic risks are all downward: contrast reduced for "softness"
([color I1](../skills/color-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), functional text shrunk to
keep a page airy ([I7](../skills/typography-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)), hairline
borders on controls that fall below the 3:1 non-text minimum, and focus rings
removed because they interrupt the surface. Each is a defect, not a refinement.
Space is also not a substitute for grouping: proximity still has to encode
relationships ([spacing I1](../skills/spacing-taste/SKILL.md#invariant)).

## DIAGNOSTICS

1. Squint. Does one mass resolve first? If nothing does, this is emptiness.
2. Can you state two proportions the page is built on, as ratios?
3. Desaturate. Does the hierarchy survive?
4. Is there exactly one expressive voice?
5. Name a detail that rewards a second look but is not needed on the first.
6. Is anything quiet *because the idea is quiet*, or quiet because quiet was the
   default? The second is drift.

## THE LOGIC, COMPACTLY

*Brief: a six-person architecture practice, twelve completed houses, excellent
photography, clients who are private homeowners.*

Twelve projects is a small quantity of very good content, so the composition is
built on interval rather than volume: each project gets one image, one line, and a
field of shaped space that makes it read as an object rather than an item in a grid.
The ratio between image mass and text mass is held at roughly 1:3 down the page, so
the eye learns the rhythm and the one deliberate departure — the house that gets a
full bleed — registers as emphasis without a label. Two voices: a display face with
real character for the project names, a quiet grotesque for everything else, both
optically corrected at size. Metadata (year, location, area) sits on a hairline
register rather than in a card, because a box would rank it as a separate object
when it is support. No shadows, one small radius or none, two motions in the whole
page. What the dialect decided was not "make it minimal" — it was that *the work is
credible only if the presentation is as considered as the buildings*, and every
interval follows from that.
